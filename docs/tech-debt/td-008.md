# TD-008 – Expired codes, tokens and Sessions are never deleted

- **Status:** Open
- **Added:** 2026-10-11
- **Area:** OAuth (`auth.authorization_codes`, `auth.grants`, `auth.access_tokens`, `auth.refresh_tokens`, `auth.sessions`)

## Current state

Every authorization, token request and sign-in adds rows to the `auth` schema. Rows are marked as consumed, revoked or expired, but never deleted.

## Why

The rows are needed for a while after they expire: reuse detection needs consumed codes and Refresh Tokens, and the history helps when investigating incidents. How long to keep them is not decided yet, so cleanup was left out of the first version.

## Impact

- The tables grow without limit. With short-lived Access Tokens and frequent refreshes, `auth.access_tokens` grows fastest.
- Lookups stay fast, because they use unique indexes on the hashes, but storage, backups and vacuuming get slower over time.

## How to resolve

Add a background service (e.g. a `BackgroundService` with a `PeriodicTimer`) that deletes in batches:

1. Authorization Codes that expired more than a day ago. `auth.grants.AuthorizationCodeId` intentionally has no foreign key, so this does not affect Grants.
2. Access Tokens that expired more than a day ago.
3. Refresh Tokens that expired or were consumed, and Grants that are revoked or have no unexpired Refresh Token left, after a retention period.
4. Sessions that expired or were revoked.

Make the retention period configurable, and log how many rows were deleted.
